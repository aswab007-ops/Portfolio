# Design System

## Direction

A dark editorial product portfolio with asymmetric composition, generous space, and restrained cinematic motion.

## Color

- Charcoal: `#101111`
- Emerald: `#154230`
- Burgundy: `#5D1E21`
- Antique gold: `#A6824A`
- Soft cream: `#E6E2DA`

Charcoal is the page foundation. Emerald carries product confidence, burgundy creates section-level drama, gold is the single interaction accent, and cream provides readable contrast.

## Typography

DM Sans is used for interface and body copy. Playfair Display Italic is limited to one editorial emphasis in the hero.

## Shape

Cards and media use a consistent 14px radius. Buttons and the floating navigation use full-pill geometry.

## Motion

Motion reveals hierarchy through staggered load, viewport entry, restrained tilt, and magnetic CTAs. All motion collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Layout

Desktop layouts are asymmetric and image-led. Every multi-column composition collapses to a single column below tablet width.
