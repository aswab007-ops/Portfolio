# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters, engineering teams, founders, and clients evaluating Muhammad Aswab Khalil's product thinking and technical range.

## Product Purpose

Present a focused body of web, mobile, and applied AI/ML work that earns a conversation.

## Positioning

A web developer and AI/ML engineer who turns complex ideas into thoughtful, useful products.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: View work
- Secondary CTA: Email
- Memorable line: Thoughtful interfaces and intelligent systems.
- Proof: Shipped projects, public repositories, production deployments, and contributor work.

## Brand Personality

Precise, sophisticated, and quietly confident.

## Anti-references

Generic developer templates, neon hacker styling, dense skill clouds, inflated metrics, and animation without purpose.

## Design Principles

- Show real work before making claims.
- Keep the experience memorable without slowing it down.
- Let design and engineering reinforce each other.
- Make technical range easy to scan.
- Respect accessibility from the first interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-friendly navigation, semantic structure, reduced-motion support, and responsive layouts.
